How the Deep Dust Clean Works

Six tones in one pass, from low bass to high treble.

Frequency Sweep

The dust mode plays six tones in a row, 200, 300, 450, 700, 1000 and 1500 Hz, for ten seconds each. Low tones shake big bits loose, while high tones reach fine dust that sits deep in the grille. The full sweep takes just one minute to finish.

When to Use It

Use deep clean when sound turns dull, quiet, or flat after weeks in a pocket or a bag. It also helps after beach trips, dusty rooms, or long days spent outdoors. If voices sound far away, built up dust is often the cause.

Getting the Best Result

Set volume to maximum, take off any case that covers the speaker, and hold the phone with the speaker facing down on a table. Run the sweep twice in a row if the sound is still dull. Then play music and listen for clear highs and bass.

Keep Every Speaker in Your Life Clean

Care tips for your phone, earbuds and more.

Earbuds and Bluetooth Speakers Too

Dust gets into earbuds and small Bluetooth speakers just as fast as it gets into your phone. The same sweep works for them too, since sound waves do not care about size at all. Place the earbud or speaker on a table, speaker side down, and run the sweep. Keep the volume high and the device still for the full minute.

Prevent Dust Before It Settles

Stop dust before it builds up in the grille. Keep your phone out of dusty pockets and bags, and wipe the grille with a dry, soft cloth each week. A good case with a speaker cover helps a lot. Run a quick dust clean once a week, even when the sound seems fine, so dust never gets a chance to settle in deep.

When the Speaker Needs Repair

If sound stays muffled after three full sweeps, the problem may be more than dust. Water damage, a torn membrane, or loose parts inside can cause weak sound. Try vibration mode once more, then test with a call. If nothing helps, take the phone to a repair shop and ask for a speaker check. Do not open the phone yourself, since that can void the warranty and cause harm.
