AirPods Speaker Cleaner Process

Cleaning AirPods with sound is simple. The tone plays from your phone, and your only job is to set the AirPods up right before you press Start.

Open the Tool

Open this page in Safari on your iPhone. The tool runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to make.

Take Your AirPods Out

Take both AirPods out of your ears. The cleaning tone plays at full volume, so it must never play through AirPods while you wear them.

Disconnect AirPods

Turn off Bluetooth, or choose iPhone as the sound output, so the tone plays from your phone speaker and not from your AirPods.

Place AirPods Nearby

Put the AirPods on a flat table, close to the bottom speaker of your phone, with the speaker mesh facing the phone. Open the case lid if they sit in the case.

Set Volume to Maximum

Turn the phone volume all the way up. A louder tone shakes dust loose from the mesh more strongly.

Play Cleaning Sound and Test

Tap Start Cleaning and let the tone finish. Then put the AirPods back in and play music. If the sound is clear, you are done. If it is still dull, brush the mesh gently and run the tone once more.

How to Remove Dust from Your AirPods Speaker

Earwax, pocket lint and dust build up slowly on the AirPods mesh, so the sound turns quiet a little at a time. Sound can shake that dust loose without opening the AirPods.

Use a Speaker Cleaning Tool

Open this page and use the cleaning tone. It plays from your phone and shakes dust loose from the mesh.

Increase Volume Fully

Set the phone volume to maximum before you start. A quiet tone cannot shake dust that has settled deep in the mesh.

Place AirPods Near the Phone

Keep the AirPods close to the bottom speaker of your phone, with the mesh facing the phone. Sound needs to reach the mesh to work.

Run the Sound for One Minute

Let the full tone finish. Stopping early leaves the last part of the sweep unplayed.

Brush the Mesh Gently

Use a dry, soft brush on the mesh after the tone. Never push a pin, needle, or SIM tool into the mesh, and never use water on it.

Test the Speaker Output

Put the AirPods back in and play a song you know well. If one side is still quiet, clean that side again and test it alone.
