How Vibration Mode Works

Sound and motion working together to free trapped water.

Dual Action

Vibration mode pairs an 80 Hz bass tone with your phone's vibration motor. The tone shakes the speaker from the inside, while the motor adds shake from the outside. Together they move water that one method alone cannot shift, even from deep inside.

Best for Submerged Phones

If your phone went under water, vibration mode is your best first step. The motor shakes hard enough to push out drops stuck deep in the grille. Use it right away, before water dries and leaves dirt behind.

How to Hold Your Phone

Hold the phone loosely in your palm, with the speaker facing down toward the floor. Do not grip it tight, since a tight grip blocks the free shake. Let the phone move a little with each pulse, so water can fall out.

Phone Fell in Water? What to Do Next

When sound cleaning is enough, and when it is not.

Act Fast, Skip the Rice

Rice cannot fix a wet speaker. It takes a full day or more, and cannot pull water out of the grille. Vibration mode works in 30 seconds, right on the spot. Switch the phone off if you can, wipe the outside dry, then run vibration with the speaker down. Repeat twice if the sound is still low. Fast action matters more than any home trick, so do not wait for rice.

Repair Costs and Insurance

A repair shop can charge 50 to 150 dollars to open a phone and clean the speaker. Some shops charge even more if parts need to be swapped. Check your phone insurance or warranty first, since water damage may be covered. Keep the bill and photos of the damage for your claim form. Our free tool costs nothing to try, so always run it before you pay for a repair.

Know Your Waterproof Rating

Most new phones have a water rating, such as IP68, which means they can handle a short dip in water. But the rating is tested in clean lab water, not in pools, sea water or soap. A rating does not mean the speaker will stay clear. After any dip, run the cleaner even if the phone seems fine, since water can hide inside.
