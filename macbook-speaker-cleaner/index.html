MacBook Speaker Cleaner Process

Cleaning a MacBook speaker with sound is simple. The tone does the work, and your only job is to set the MacBook up right before you press Start.

Open the Tool

Open this page in Safari or Chrome on your MacBook. The tool runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to make.

Set Volume to Maximum

Turn the volume all the way up and switch off mute. A louder tone shakes the speaker harder and pushes more dust out.

Keep the Lid Open

Keep the lid open and put the MacBook on a flat table. Closing the lid puts the MacBook to sleep, and the sound stops.

Play Cleaning Sound

Tap Start Cleaning. The tool plays a low 165 Hz tone that makes the speaker shake and loosen dust in the grille.

Let It Run for 50 to 60 Seconds

Leave the MacBook still until the bar reaches 100 percent. Stopping early leaves the shake unfinished.

Test Speaker Sound

Play music or a video. If the sound is clear, you are done. If it is still dull, run the dust sweep next.

How to Use the MacBook Speaker Cleaner

Use the tool in your browser, with the MacBook on a table, the lid open, and the volume at maximum. Pick Remove Dust first, because dust is the main problem on a MacBook. The left and right buttons clean one side at a time, and Both Speakers cleans both sides together. Run the sweep two or three times for a bad case, and test with music after each run. If the MacBook had a spill, power it off and dry the outside first, and only use the tool for light moisture after that.

How to Remove Dust from Your MacBook Speaker

Dust from the keyboard and the desk builds up slowly in the speaker grille, so the sound turns quiet a little at a time. Sound can shake that dust loose without opening the MacBook.

Use a Speaker Cleaning Tool

Open this page and use the dust sweep. It plays tones from 200 Hz to 1500 Hz, so each step shakes dust of a different size loose.

Increase Volume Fully

Set the volume to maximum before you start. A quiet tone cannot shake dust that has settled deep in the grille.

Keep the Lid Open

Keep the lid open while the tone plays. The speakers sit beside the keyboard or along the sides, and the sound needs the lid open to reach them.

Run the Sound for One Minute

Let the full sweep finish. Each tone runs for ten seconds, and stopping early leaves the high tones unplayed.

Avoid Sharp Objects

Never push a pin, needle, or any sharp object into the grille. Sharp objects tear the thin mesh and can damage the speaker behind it.

Test the Speaker Output

Play a song you know well and listen for clear highs and full volume. If dust is still there, wipe the grille with a dry, soft brush and run the sweep again.
