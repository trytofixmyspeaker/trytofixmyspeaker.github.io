Laptop Speakers Not Working? Fixes for Windows and Mac

Your laptop speakers have gone silent, and no sound comes out no matter what you play. This is a common problem on Windows and Mac, and most of the time the fix is a setting or a driver. Work through these steps in order, and test the sound after each one.

Why Do Laptop Speakers Stop Working?

Laptop speakers stop working for a few common reasons. The output device can switch to the wrong place, like a monitor or Bluetooth headphones. The audio driver can break after a system update, the audio service can stop, or the speakers themselves can fail.

Start with the quick checks. Many sound problems take less than a minute to fix, and you do not need to open the laptop or install anything strange. Only move to the driver steps if the quick checks do not help.

Step 1: Check the Volume and the Mute Key

Look at the speaker icon and make sure the volume is up and the sound is not muted. Most laptops have a mute key on the keyboard, often with a small light, and it is easy to press it by accident. Press it once and watch the light go off.

Also check the volume inside the app you are using. A video player or a call app can be muted on its own, even when the system sound is on. Turn the app volume up and play a test sound.

Step 2: Check the Output Device

On Windows, right-click the speaker icon in the taskbar and open Sound settings. Look at the output device list and make sure your laptop speakers are selected. Windows can switch the output to a monitor, a TV, or Bluetooth headphones without asking you.

On a Mac, open System Settings, go to Sound, and check the Output list. Make sure the built-in speakers are selected, and set the output volume to a normal level. If the speakers are not in the list, restart the Mac and check again.

Step 3: Restart the Laptop

Restart the laptop from the Start menu on Windows, or from the Apple menu on a Mac. A full restart clears stuck audio processes and lets the system reload the sound. This fixes more sound problems than people expect.

After the restart, play a video or a song. If the sound is back, the problem was a stuck process. If it is still silent, move on to the next step.

Step 4: Test with Headphones

Plug in headphones or earbuds and play a sound. If you hear sound in the headphones but not from the speakers, the problem is with the built-in speakers or their connection, not with the system. If there is no sound anywhere, the problem is in the software.

Try a different pair of headphones and a different file, to rule out a bad pair or a single bad audio file.

Step 5: Run the Sound Troubleshooter (Windows)

On Windows, open Settings, go to System, then Sound, and look for Troubleshoot. Windows will check the output device, the driver, and the audio service, and it will offer a fix if it finds one. Let it finish before you close the window.

Note what it finds if it cannot fix the problem, because that points to the next step.

Step 6: Update or Reinstall the Audio Driver (Windows)

Open Device Manager, expand Sound, video and game controllers, and right-click your audio device. Choose Update driver and let Windows search for a newer one. A broken driver is one of the most common causes of no sound after a Windows update.

If updating does not help, look for Roll back driver, which returns to the earlier version. If there is no rollback option, choose Uninstall device and restart, and Windows will install the driver again. Only get drivers from Windows Update or the laptop maker's official support website.

Step 7: Check the Audio Service (Windows) and Sound Settings (Mac)

On Windows, press the Windows key and R, type services.msc, and press Enter. Find Windows Audio in the list and check that it is running. If it is stopped, right-click it and choose Start. Also check Windows Audio Endpoint Builder the same way.

On a Mac, open System Settings, go to Sound, and check that the output is set to the built-in speakers and the volume is up. Also install the latest macOS updates. If the sound is still missing, reset the NVRAM, which stores sound settings. Apple publishes the steps for your model on its support website.

Step 8: Check the BIOS (Windows Laptops)

You can also check the BIOS, which is the basic setup screen of the laptop. Restart the laptop and press the key shown on the screen to enter it. Look for an audio setting and make sure it is enabled. Do not change anything else in the BIOS, and save and exit when you are done.

When It Is Time to See a Professional

If the sound works in headphones but not from the speakers, and the driver, service, and settings steps did not help, the speakers or their cable may be damaged. A drop, a spill, or wear over time can damage the small parts inside. Take the laptop to a repair shop, and tell them what you already tried, so they can test the speakers directly.

Final Guidance

No sound from laptop speakers is usually a setting, a driver, or a stopped service. Check the volume, the output device, and restart first. Then test with headphones, run the troubleshooter, and check the driver. Only think about hardware when the sound works in headphones but not from the speakers, because that points to the speakers themselves.
