iPhone Speaker Not Working? 9 Fixes That Work

Is your iPhone speaker not working, with calls silent and videos playing without sound? The cause is often a simple setting or a blocked grille, and most fixes take less than a minute. Work through these nine fixes in order, and test the sound after each one, so you find the cause fast.

Why Is Your iPhone Speaker Not Working?

An iPhone speaker stops working for many small reasons, and most of them are easy to fix at home. The silent switch may be on, the volume may be low, or the sound may be going to a Bluetooth device you forgot about. A case can cover the grille, a Focus mode can silence calls, and dust can build up in the small holes at the bottom of the phone.

Sometimes the cause is software. A glitch can silence the speaker until you restart the phone, and an old version of iOS can carry a sound bug that a newer update fixes.

Fix 1: Check the Silent Switch on the Side

Look at the small switch on the left side of your iPhone, just above the volume buttons. If you can see an orange line on it, the phone is on silent, and ringtones and alerts will not play through the speaker. Flip the switch back so the orange line is hidden, then test the sound again. On newer models, check the Action Button or the Control Centre instead.

Fix 2: Raise the Volume with the Buttons

Press the volume up button on the side while a video or a song is playing. The iPhone keeps a separate volume for media, ringtones, and calls, so the volume can be fine for one and silent for another. You can also open the Control Centre and drag the volume slider up.

Fix 3: Turn Bluetooth Off

Open Settings, go to Bluetooth, and check what is connected. AirPods, a car stereo, or a smart speaker can stay connected after you stop using them, and the iPhone keeps sending sound to them. Turn Bluetooth off and play a song. If the sound comes back, that was the cause.

Fix 4: Turn Off Focus or Do Not Disturb

Open the Control Centre and look for a moon icon or a Focus label. Do Not Disturb and other Focus modes silence calls and alerts, so the phone can seem broken when it is only quiet on purpose. Tap the Focus icon to turn it off, then test the sound again.

Fix 5: Remove a Case or Screen Protector Covering a Speaker

Take the case off and play a song. Many cases cover the bottom grille by a small amount, and that is enough to make the sound dull or silent. A screen protector can also hang over the earpiece slit at the top. If the sound comes back with the case off, the case was the problem.

Fix 6: Gently Clean the Speaker Grille with a Soft Brush

Look at the bottom grille. If you see dust or lint in the holes, brush it gently with a soft brush. Never use a pin or a needle, because sharp tools can damage the speaker behind the mesh. You can also clean the grille with sound, using our iPhone Speaker Cleaner, Deep Clean, or Vibration tones.

Fix 7: Restart the iPhone

A restart clears small software glitches that can silence the speaker. Hold the side button and one volume button together, slide to power off, wait, then hold the side button again to turn the phone back on. If that does not help, try a force restart. Press volume up, then volume down, then hold the side button until the Apple logo appears.

Fix 8: Test Sound in Different Apps and with Speakerphone

Play music in one app, then a video in another app, then make a short call. If the sound is silent in every app, the speaker or its grille is the problem. If only one app is silent, that app is the cause. Also try the speakerphone and the normal earpiece, as our guide on how to test if your phone speaker is damaged explains.

Fix 9: Update iOS, Then Reset All Settings as a Last Step

Open Settings, go to General, then Software Update, and install any update that is waiting. An old version of iOS can carry a sound bug, and a newer update can fix it. If the update does not help, reset all settings as a last step. Go to Settings, General, Transfer or Reset iPhone, then Reset All Settings. This removes saved Wi-Fi passwords, so write one down first.

When It Is Time to See a Repair Shop

If you worked through all nine fixes and the speaker is still silent, the speaker itself may be damaged. A torn membrane, a loose part, or water damage inside the phone will not improve with cleaning or a reset. Take the phone to a repair shop, and tell them what you already tried. For more help, read our guide on phone speaker not working for other phones too.

Final Guidance

An iPhone speaker that stops working is usually a problem with a quick fix. Check the silent switch, raise the volume, rule out Bluetooth and Focus, and clean the grille before you worry about damage. Most iPhones sound clear again after one of these steps. If nothing helps, a repair shop can test the speaker and tell you the real cause.
