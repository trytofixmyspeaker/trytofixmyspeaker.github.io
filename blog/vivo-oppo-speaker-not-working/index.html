Vivo or Oppo Speaker Not Working? Fix It

If your vivo oppo speaker not working problem started today, do not worry yet. In most cases the cause is simple, and you can fix it at home in a few minutes. This guide covers both Vivo and Oppo phones together, because the steps are the same. Work through them in order, and test the sound after each step.

Vivo Oppo Speaker Not Working: The Common Causes

A Vivo or Oppo speaker can go silent for many small reasons. The media volume may be down, Bluetooth may still be sending sound to earbuds, Do Not Disturb may be silencing calls, or dust may be blocking the grille. The steps below check each cause. If your speaker is silent in every app, read our guide on a phone speaker not working too.

Step 1: Raise the Volume and Check Mute

Press the volume up button while music or a video is playing. Media, call, and ringtone volumes are separate, so the ringtone can be loud while the media volume is still low. Open the volume panel, raise every slider, and check that the phone is not muted.

Step 2: Turn Bluetooth Off

Your phone may still be connected to earbuds, a speaker, or a car stereo. The sound then goes to that device, and the phone speaker stays quiet. Turn Bluetooth off in quick settings, then play a song. If the sound comes back, a forgotten Bluetooth device was the cause.

Step 3: Turn Off Do Not Disturb

Do Not Disturb can silence calls, messages, and alerts. Turn off Do Not Disturb in the quick settings panel. After that, ask someone to call you. If the sound returns, the mode was the cause.

Step 4: Remove the Case or Screen Protector

Take the case off and play a song. Many cases cover the bottom speaker grille a little, and that is enough to make the sound quiet or dull. A screen protector can also hang over the earpiece slit at the top. If the sound turns clear with the case off, the case is the problem.

Step 5: Gently Clean the Speaker Grille

Look at the speaker grille in good light, where dust and lint collect in the small holes. Brush the grille very gently with a soft brush, and wipe the area with a dry cloth. Never push a pin or a needle into the holes, because a sharp tool can tear the mesh and damage the speaker behind it. You can also clean the grille with sound. Open our Deep Clean tool, or try Vibration mode.

Step 6: Restart the Phone

A restart clears short term faults. Press and hold the power button, then tap Restart. If there is no restart option, turn it off, wait 30 seconds, and turn it back on. After it starts again, play a song and make a short test call.

Step 7: Try Safe Mode to Rule Out a Bad App

Safe mode starts the phone with only the built in apps, so it shows you whether a downloaded app is the cause. To enter safe mode, press and hold the power button, then press and hold Power off until the safe mode prompt appears. In safe mode, play a sound or make a call. If the speaker works, a downloaded app is likely the cause, so restart the phone to leave safe mode and remove apps you installed just before the problem started.

Step 8: Test Sound in Different Apps and with Speakerphone

Play music in one app, then a video in another, then make a short call. If the sound is missing everywhere, the speaker or its grille is the problem. If only one app has no sound, check its own volume, update it, or reinstall it. For a full test method, read our guide on how to test if your phone speaker is damaged. On a call, try speakerphone, then switch back to the normal earpiece.

Step 9: Check the Sound Settings

Open Settings and look at the sound options. On a Vivo phone, look for the Sound menu in Settings. On an Oppo phone, look for the Sound and vibration menu. The exact words can change a little from one model to another, so look for the word Sound. That is the only real difference between the two brands here. Check the volume sliders, and turn off any extra sound effects.

Step 10: Update the System Software and Apps

Old software can carry a sound fault that an update fixes. Open Settings, go to System update or Software update, and check for a new update. If one is available, connect to Wi-Fi, charge the phone, and install it. Also update your apps in the app store. After the updates, restart the phone and test the sound.

When to See a Repair Shop

If you worked through every step and the speaker is still silent, it may be damaged. A torn speaker, a loose part, or water damage will not improve with cleaning or settings. Do not open the phone yourself. Take it to a repair shop and tell them what you already tried.

Final Guidance

A silent Vivo or Oppo speaker is often a small problem. Check the volume, turn Bluetooth off, turn off Do Not Disturb, remove the case, and clean the grille. Restart the phone, test the sound in different apps, and update the software.
