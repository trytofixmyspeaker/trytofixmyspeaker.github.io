Xiaomi Clear Speaker Not Showing? What to Do

The Clear Speaker option is missing on your Xiaomi phone, and you cannot find it in Settings. This is common, and it does not always mean something is wrong with your phone. This guide explains why the option is missing and what you can try instead, step by step.

What Is the Clear Speaker Feature on Xiaomi?

Clear Speaker is a built-in tool on some Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO phones. It plays a loud tone for about 30 seconds, and the sound shakes water and loose dust out of the speaker grille. You would use it after the phone gets wet, or when the speaker starts to sound muffled. It works the same way as the free tool on this site.

The feature lives in Settings, under Additional settings, on the phones that have it. But not every Xiaomi phone has it. Xiaomi adds and removes small features like this in software updates, and some models never get it at all. So a missing option is often normal, and it does not mean your phone is broken or fake.

Step 1: Search Settings for Clear Speaker

Open Settings and tap the search bar at the top. Type "Clear Speaker" and wait for the results. If the option appears, tap it and follow the steps on the screen. This is the fastest way to find it, because the setting can sit in a different place on different software versions.

If nothing appears in the search, the feature is probably not on your phone. Do not download random apps that promise to add it, because many of them show ads or ask for permissions they do not need. The steps below give you safe options instead.

Step 2: Look in Additional Settings

Open Settings, scroll down, and tap Additional settings. Look through the list for Clear Speaker. On some phones it sits near the bottom, and on others it is grouped with other tools. Take your time and read each item.

If it is not there, check the sound settings too. Open Settings, tap Sound and vibration, and look for anything about the speaker. The feature has moved between menus in past updates, so it is worth a quick look in both places.

Step 3: Update Your Phone Software

Open Settings, tap About phone, and check for a software update. Xiaomi sometimes adds the Clear Speaker feature in an update, and sometimes an update moves it to a new place. Install any update that is waiting, then restart the phone and search for the option again.

Make sure the phone is charged and connected to Wi-Fi before you update. An update can take a few minutes, so do not turn the phone off while it is running.

Step 4: Restart Your Phone

A simple restart can fix a setting that is not showing up. Press and hold the power button, tap Restart, and wait for the phone to turn back on. Then open Settings and search for Clear Speaker again.

A restart also clears small software glitches that can hide menu items. It takes less than a minute, and it is safe to try before anything else.

Step 5: Check If Your Model Has the Feature

Not every Xiaomi, Redmi, or POCO phone has Clear Speaker. Budget models and older phones are more likely to miss it, and some phones sold in certain regions do not have it either. This is normal, and it says nothing about the quality of your phone.

You can check the user guide for your model on Xiaomi's official website, or ask on the official community forum. Do not trust a random video or post that says every Xiaomi phone has the feature, because that is not true.

Step 6: Use a Browser Speaker Cleaner Instead

You do not need the built-in option to clean your speaker with sound. Open our free Speaker Cleaner in your phone browser and run the water eject tone. It plays the same kind of sound, and it works on any phone, Xiaomi or not. No app to install, and no sign-up.

Hold the phone with the speaker facing down while the tone plays, so water and dust can fall out. Run it two or three times if the speaker still sounds muffled, and dry the outside of the phone with a soft cloth first.

Step 7: Clean the Speaker Grille by Hand

Look at the speaker grille in good light. If you see dust or lint in the holes, brush it gently with a soft brush and wipe the area with a dry cloth. Do not push a pin or a needle into the holes, because sharp tools tear the mesh and can damage the speaker behind it.

If your phone got wet, dry the outside first and let it sit for a while before you play sound. Do not charge a wet phone, and do not use a hair dryer, because hot air can push water deeper inside. If the sound is muffled in every app, read our guide on how to fix a muffled phone speaker for more checks.

When It Is Time to See a Professional

If the speaker still sounds muffled after cleaning, and the sound is bad in every app, the speaker itself may be damaged. Water, a drop, or wear over time can damage the small parts inside. Take the phone to a repair shop, and tell them what you already tried, so they can start with the speaker itself.

Final Guidance

A missing Clear Speaker option is usually not a fault. The feature is not on every Xiaomi phone, and updates can move it or remove it. Search Settings first, check for an update, and restart the phone. If it is still missing, use the free browser tool on this site to clean your speaker with sound. It does the same job, and it works on any phone.
