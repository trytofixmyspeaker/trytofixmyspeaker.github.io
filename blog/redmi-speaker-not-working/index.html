Redmi Speaker Not Working? Fixes for HyperOS

A silent Redmi speaker is stressful, because you can miss calls, alarms, and messages without knowing it. The good news is that the cause is often simple. Work through these safe steps in order, and test the sound after each one, so you can find the problem fast.

Why Is Your Redmi Speaker Not Working?

A Redmi speaker usually stops working for a small reason. The volume may be down, the sound may be going to a Bluetooth device, or Do Not Disturb may be silencing alerts. Dust, a case, or a bad app can cause the same trouble.

These steps work on Redmi phones running HyperOS, and on most Xiaomi phones too, because the settings and menus are very similar. Start with the easy checks, and move on only if the sound is still missing.

Step 1: Raise the Volume and Check Mute

Press the volume up button while a song is playing, and watch the volume bar. Phones keep separate levels for media, calls, ringtones, and alarms, so the home screen volume may not be the one you need. Open Settings, then Sound and vibration, check each slider, and make sure the phone is not on mute.

Step 2: Turn Bluetooth Off

Open the quick settings panel, turn Bluetooth off, and play a song. Earbuds, a car stereo, or a smart speaker can stay connected after you stop using them, and the phone keeps sending sound to them. The speaker then stays silent, even though nothing is wrong with it.

Step 3: Turn Off Do Not Disturb

Open the settings panel and look for Do Not Disturb. When it is on, calls and alerts stay silent, and it is easy to miss that it is still active after a meeting. Turn it off and ask someone to call you. Check sleep schedules too, because one can turn it back on daily.

Step 4: Remove a Case or Screen Protector Covering a Speaker

Take the case off and play a song. Many cases cover the bottom grille a little, and that is enough to make the sound weak or silent. A screen protector can also hang over the earpiece slit at the top, which muffles calls. If the sound comes back, get a case with a proper speaker cutout.

Step 5: Gently Clean the Speaker Grille

Look at the bottom grille in good light. If you see dust or lint in the holes, brush it gently with a soft brush. Never push a pin, a needle, or a toothpick into the holes, because sharp tools can tear the mesh and damage the speaker behind it. Sound can help too. Try our Deep Clean tones or Vibration mode.

Step 6: Restart the Phone

Press and hold the power button, then tap Restart. A restart clears small software glitches that can leave the speaker silent, and it is one of the fastest fixes on any phone. Wait for the phone to start up fully, then play a song to test the sound.

Step 7: Try Safe Mode to Rule Out a Bad App

Safe mode runs only the system apps, and turns off the apps you installed. If the speaker works in safe mode, one of your apps is the cause. To enter safe mode, hold the power button, then hold the Power off option until the safe mode prompt appears, and tap OK. Test the sound, then restart the phone normally to leave safe mode.

Step 8: Test Sound in Different Apps and with Speakerphone

Play music in one app, then a video in another, then make a short call. If sound is missing everywhere, the speaker or its settings are the problem. If only one app is silent, that app is the cause, so check its settings or reinstall it. See our phone speaker not working guide for more on this test. On a call, switch to speakerphone. If speakerphone works but the earpiece is silent, the earpiece is the problem.

Step 9: Check Sound Settings in HyperOS

Open Settings, then Sound and vibration, and go through each option slowly. Check the media, ringtone, alarm, and call volume sliders, and make sure no volume limit is keeping the sound low. Turn off any sound effects or equalizer presets, because a wrong preset can make the sound very quiet.

Step 10: Update HyperOS and Your Apps

Open Settings, then About phone, and check for a system update. A software bug can break the speaker after an update, and a later update can fix it, so install any update that is waiting. Then open the Play Store and update your apps, especially music, video, and calling apps.

When to See a Repair Shop

If you worked through every step and the speaker is still silent, the speaker itself may be damaged. A torn membrane, water damage, or a loose part inside will not improve with cleaning or settings changes. See our guide on how to test if your phone speaker is damaged before you go. Take it to a repair shop and tell them what you already tried. Do not open the phone yourself, because that can void the warranty.

Final Guidance

A silent Redmi speaker is usually a small problem. Check the volume, turn off Bluetooth and Do Not Disturb, remove the case, clean the grille gently, and restart the phone before you worry about damage. Most phones make sound again after one step. If nothing helps, a repair shop can test the speaker and tell you the real cause.
