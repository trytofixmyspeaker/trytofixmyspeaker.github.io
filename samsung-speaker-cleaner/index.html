Samsung Speaker Cleaner Process

Cleaning a Galaxy speaker with sound is simple, and the setup matters more than the tone. A phone placed the right way lets gravity help the sound push water and dust out of the grille. The whole process takes about a minute, and you can repeat it if the sound is still dull.

Open the Tool

Open this page in the browser on your Galaxy phone. The tool runs on the page itself, so there is nothing to install and no account to make.

Set Volume to Maximum

Turn the volume all the way up. A louder tone shakes the speaker harder, and a hard shake pushes more water and dust out of the grille.

Place Your Galaxy Face Down

Put the phone face down on a flat surface, with the bottom speaker grille pointing down. On most Galaxy phones the grille sits at the bottom edge, beside the USB-C port.

Play the Cleaning Sound

Tap Start Cleaning. The tool plays a low 165 Hz tone that makes the speaker shake and push water drops out through the grille.

Let It Run for 50 to 60 Seconds

Leave the phone still until the bar reaches 100 percent. Picking it up early stops the shake before the water has moved out.

Test Your Speaker Sound

Play music or make a short call. If the sound is clear, you are done. If it is still dull, run the tone once more, then try the dust sweep.

How to Remove Dust from Your Samsung Speaker

Pocket lint, dust, and sand build up slowly in the bottom grille, so the sound turns quiet a little at a time. Many Galaxy owners first notice it on calls, when voices sound far away. Sound can shake that dust loose without opening the phone.

Use a Samsung Speaker Cleaning Tool

Open this page and use the dust sweep. It plays tones from 200 Hz to 1500 Hz, so each step shakes dust of a different size loose from the grille.

Increase Volume to Maximum

Set the volume to maximum before you start. A quiet tone cannot shake dust that has settled deep in the grille after weeks in a pocket.

Place Your Samsung Phone Face Down

Hold the phone with the bottom speaker facing down. Gravity helps the loose dust fall out instead of falling back into the grille.

Run the Cleaning Sound for One Minute

Let the full sweep finish. Each tone runs for ten seconds, and stopping early leaves the high tones unplayed, so fine dust stays behind.

Avoid Using Sharp Objects

Never push a pin, needle, or SIM tool into the grille. Sharp objects tear the thin mesh and can damage the speaker behind it, which sound cannot fix.

Test Your Speaker Performance

Play a song you know well and listen for clear highs and full volume. If dust is still there, wipe the grille with a dry, soft brush and run the sweep again.
