The tool works on most devices with speakers and a browser. How well it works can differ by device type and age.

Phones and tablets work best, since their speakers are small and close to the grille. Keep the volume high and the speaker down.

For Laptops and Computers:

Laptops work too, but their speakers are bigger and set back. Place the laptop on a flat desk and run the sweep twice.

For Wireless Speakers and Headphones:

Small Bluetooth speakers and earbuds can also use the sweep. Keep them still, and do not use vibration mode on tiny earbuds.
