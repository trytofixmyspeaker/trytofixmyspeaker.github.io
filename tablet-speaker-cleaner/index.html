Tablet Speaker Cleaner Process

Cleaning a tablet speaker with sound is simple. The tone plays from the tablet, and your only job is to set the tablet up right before you press Start.

Open the Tool

Open this page in the browser on your tablet. The tool runs in the browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to make.

Take the Tablet Out of Its Case

Take the tablet out of a thick case, or open the case cover, so nothing covers the speaker grille. A case can block the sound and trap the dust inside.

Place the Speaker Facing Down

Hold the tablet with the speaker grille facing down, over a table or your hand. Gravity helps the dust and water fall out while the tone plays.

Set Volume to Maximum

Turn the tablet volume all the way up. A louder tone shakes dust loose from the grille more strongly.

Play Cleaning Sound and Test

Tap Start Cleaning and let the tone finish. Then play music or a video you know well. If the sound is clear, you are done. If it is still dull, wipe the grille gently and run the tone once more.

How to Use the Tablet Speaker Cleaner

Use the tool on the tablet itself, with the speaker facing down and the volume at maximum. Pick Eject Water first if the tablet got wet, and Remove Dust when the sound turned dull from daily use. Run the tone two or three times for a bad case, wipe the grille gently between runs, and test with music after each run.

How to Remove Dust from Your Tablet Speaker

Dust, pocket lint and crumbs build up slowly in the tablet speaker grille, so the sound turns quiet a little at a time. Sound can shake that dust loose without opening the tablet.

Use a Speaker Cleaning Tool

Open this page on the tablet and use the cleaning tone. It plays from the tablet speaker and shakes dust loose from the grille.

Increase Volume Fully

Set the tablet volume to maximum before you start. A quiet tone cannot shake dust that has settled deep in the grille.

Place the Tablet Speaker Down

Keep the speaker grille facing down while the tone plays. Dust needs somewhere to fall out to.

Run the Sound for One Minute

Let the full tone finish. Stopping early leaves the last part of the sweep unplayed.

Wipe the Grille Gently

Use a dry, soft cloth or a dry, soft brush on the grille after the tone. Never push a pin, needle, or SIM tool into the grille, and never use water on it.

Test the Speaker Output

Play a song or video you know well. If one side is still quiet, clean that side again and test it alone.

What Is a Tablet Speaker Cleaner?

A tablet speaker cleaner is a sound tool that clears the tablet speaker grille without opening the tablet. It plays tones from the tablet speaker. The low tone shakes the grille, the sweep shakes dust of different sizes loose, and vibration mode adds a shake for dirt that sound alone cannot move. It works on the small grille holes where sound comes out, which is where dust collects.

Why a Tablet Speaker Cleaner Matters

A tablet carries video, lessons, calls and games, so a blocked speaker affects the whole day. The grille sits on the edge of the tablet, so it picks up dust from bags, tables and hands fast. A quick sound clean, plus a gentle wipe, keeps the sound loud and clear, and it costs nothing, unlike a replacement or a repair shop visit.
