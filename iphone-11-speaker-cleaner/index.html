Common iPhone 11 Speaker Problems

The iPhone 11 has been in daily use for years now, so its speaker problems are easy to recognise. Most of them come from the same two causes, water and dust, and sound can fix both.

Calls Sound Quiet in the Earpiece

You hold the phone to your ear and the caller sounds far away, even at full volume. Dust in the small earpiece grille at the top of the phone is usually the cause.

Music Sounds Muffled and Flat

Songs lose their bass and voices sound dull. Lint in the bottom speaker grille beside the Lightning port blocks the sound before it can leave the phone.

The Speaker Crackles at Low Volume

At low volume you hear a crackle behind the music. A drop of water or a grain of dust sitting on the speaker membrane can cause it.

Clean Your iPhone 11 Speaker in 6 Steps

Cleaning an iPhone 11 speaker with sound is simple. The tone does the work, and your only job is to set the phone up right before you press Start.

Open the Page in Safari

Open this page in Safari or Chrome on your iPhone 11. The tool runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to make.

Turn the Volume All the Way Up

Turn the volume all the way up and switch off silent mode. A louder tone shakes the speaker harder and pushes more water and dust out.

Lay the Phone Face Down

Put the phone face down on a flat surface, with the speaker grille pointing down. On the iPhone 11 the speaker grille sits beside the Lightning port, at the bottom edge.

Tap Start and Listen for the Tone

Tap Start Cleaning. The tool plays a low 165 Hz tone that makes the speaker shake and push water drops out through the grille.

Wait Until the Bar Hits 100 Percent

Leave the phone still until the bar reaches 100 percent. Picking it up early stops the shake before the water has moved out.

Play Music and Check the Sound

Play music or make a short call. If the sound is clear, you are done. If it is still dull, run the tone once more.

Get Dust Out of Your iPhone 11 Speaker

Pocket lint and dust build up slowly in the speaker grille, so the sound turns quiet a little at a time. Sound can shake that dust loose without opening the phone.

Open the Dust Sweep

Open this page and use the dust sweep. It plays tones from 200 Hz to 1500 Hz, so each step shakes dust of a different size loose.

Turn the Volume to Maximum

Set the volume to maximum before you start. A quiet tone cannot shake dust that has settled deep in the grille.

Hold the Phone Speaker Down

Hold the phone with the speaker facing down. Gravity helps the loose dust fall out instead of falling back in.

Let the Full Minute Finish

Let the full sweep finish. Each tone runs for ten seconds, and stopping early leaves the high tones unplayed.

Keep Sharp Objects Away

Never push a pin, needle, or SIM tool into the grille. Sharp objects tear the thin mesh and can damage the speaker behind it.

Play a Song and Listen

Play a song you know well and listen for clear highs and full volume. If dust is still there, wipe the grille with a dry, soft brush and run the sweep again.
