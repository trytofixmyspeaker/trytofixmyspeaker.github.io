## What the tool does

- **Eject Water**: pulsed 165 Hz tone (1s on, 0.3s off) for 60 seconds pushes water drops out of the speaker grille.
- **Remove Dust**: six tones (200, 300, 450, 700, 1000, 1500 Hz) for 10 seconds each shake loose dust, lint and debris.
- **Vibration**: 80 Hz bass tone for 30 seconds plus the phone vibration motor shakes out trapped water.
- **Speakers**: clean both speakers, or left or right only, with stereo panning.
- **Progress**: live progress bar, percent, and status text, with a completion message that links to the next mode.

## How to use it

1. Turn your volume to maximum.
2. Place the phone on a flat surface, speaker facing down.
3. Pick a mode, choose a speaker, and tap Start Cleaning.
4. Let the progress bar reach 100%.
5. Test your sound. Repeat 2 or 3 times if it is still muffled.

## Works on

Any device with a speaker and a web browser: iPhone, Samsung, Pixel, Android phones, tablets, iPads and laptops.
