Laptop Speaker Cleaner Process

Cleaning a laptop speaker with sound is simple. The tone does the work, and your only job is to set the laptop up right before you press Start.

Open the Tool

Open this page in your browser on your laptop. The tool runs in the browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to make.

Set Volume to Maximum

Turn the volume all the way up and close other apps that play sound. A louder tone shakes the speaker harder and pushes more dust out.

Place the Laptop on a Flat Table

Put the laptop on a flat table, with the speaker grille clear. The grille sits beside the keyboard, or on the bottom edge, on most laptops.

Play Cleaning Sound

Tap Start Cleaning. The tool plays tones that make the speaker shake and push dust out through the grille.

Let It Run for a Few Minutes

Laptop speakers are bigger than phone speakers, so let the tone run for a few minutes. Run it two or three times for a bad case, and leave the laptop still until it finishes.

Test Speaker Sound

Play music or a video you know well. If the sound is clear, you are done. If it is still dull, run the tone once more.

How to Remove Dust from Your Laptop Speaker

Dust builds up slowly on the laptop grille, so the sound turns quiet a little at a time. Sound can shake that dust loose without opening the laptop.

Use a Speaker Cleaning Tool

Increase Volume to 100 Percent

Set the volume to maximum before you start. A quiet tone cannot shake dust that has settled deep in the grille.

Play the Dust Removal Sound

Tap Start and let the full sweep finish. Each tone runs for ten seconds, and stopping early leaves the high tones unplayed.

Let the Sound Run for a Few Minutes

Run the sweep two or three times. Laptop grilles are wider than phone grilles, so they need more time to clear.

Check Sound Clarity

Play a song you know well and listen for clear highs and full volume. If dust is still there, wipe the grille with a dry, soft brush and run the sweep again.

Repeat the Process if Needed

Repeat once more if the sound is still dull. If three runs do not help, the problem may be damage inside the laptop, not dust.

How to Remove Water from Your Laptop Speaker

Water in a laptop speaker is rare. Dust is the real problem on most laptops. But if a splash reached the grille, sound can help, if you act fast and dry the outside first.

Dry the Outside First

Turn the laptop off, unplug it, and wipe the outside dry with a soft cloth. Do not charge a wet laptop.
