What This Tool Does to Your iPhone 12 Speaker

An iPhone 12 speaker cleaner is a sound tool that clears the speaker without opening the phone. It plays tones through the speaker itself. The low 165 Hz tone pushes water out, the sweep from 200 Hz to 1500 Hz shakes dust loose, and vibration mode adds a shake for dirt that sound alone cannot move. It works on the bottom speaker, where music plays, and on the earpiece, where callers are heard. The same tool works on iPhone 12, iPhone 12 mini, iPhone 12 Pro and iPhone 12 Pro Max.

iPhone 12 Speaker Not Working? Start Here

Before you clean, check what the speaker is doing. The fault you hear tells you which mode to run first, and it tells you when the phone needs a repair shop instead.

Calls Are Hard to Hear

The caller sounds far away even at full volume. Dust in the small earpiece grille at the top of the phone is usually the cause, so run the dust sweep first.

Music Sounds Dull

Songs lose their bass and voices sound flat. Lint in the bottom speaker grille beside the Lightning port blocks the sound before it can leave the phone.

Sound Crackles at Low Volume

You hear a crackle behind the music when the volume is low. A drop of water or a grain of dust sitting on the speaker membrane can cause it, so run the water eject tone first.

No Sound at All

If there is no sound at all, check silent mode and the volume first. If the settings are fine, the speaker itself may be damaged, and sound alone will not fix it.

Run the Water Eject Sound

The water eject tone is the first step when your iPhone 12 got wet. It takes one minute, and the phone does all the work once you press Start.

Take the Case Off

Take the case off first, so nothing blocks the speaker grille. A case can trap water against the phone and stop it from falling out.

Turn the Volume to Maximum

Turn the volume all the way up and switch off silent mode. A louder tone shakes the speaker harder and pushes more water out.

Face the Speaker Down

Put the phone face down on a flat surface, with the speaker grille pointing down. On the iPhone 12 the speaker grille sits beside the Lightning port, at the bottom edge.

Tap Start and Wait

Tap Start Cleaning and leave the phone still. The tool plays a low 165 Hz tone for 60 seconds. Picking the phone up early stops the shake before the water has moved out.

Test the Sound

Play music or make a short call. If the sound is clear, you are done. If it is still dull, run the tone once more.

iPhone 12 Speaker Cleaning Sound: How the Dust Sweep Works

Pocket lint and dust build up slowly in the speaker grille, so the sound turns quiet a little at a time. The dust sweep plays six tones, from 200 Hz to 1500 Hz, and each tone runs for ten seconds. Each step shakes dust of a different size loose, so the full sweep matters more than any single tone. Hold the phone with the speaker facing down while it runs, so gravity helps the loose dust fall out instead of falling back in. Never push a pin, needle, or SIM tool into the grille, because sharp objects tear the thin mesh and can damage the speaker behind it.
