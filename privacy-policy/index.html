Privacy Policy

How we protect your privacy when you use our free speaker cleaner tool.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the small amount of data needed to run this free tool. We never ask for your name, email, or account to use it.

1.1 Personal Information

Personal information is data that points to you, like a name or email. Our tool does not collect it. The form uses only what you type.

1.2 Usage Data

Usage data is basic info about how the tool is used, such as which mode was picked. We use it only to make the tool better.

1.3 Device Information

Device information includes your browser type and screen size. It helps us fix layout bugs and keep the tool working on all devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to run the tool and fix bugs. We never use it to show ads based on who you are.

We also use it to answer your messages when you write to us. Your message is used only to reply, and never for marketing.

3. Information We Share

We do not sell your data to anyone. We share data only in the few cases listed below, and only as much as is needed.

3.1 Service Providers

Some services help us run the site, such as hosting and fonts. They see only the data they need to do their job.

3.2 Legal Requirements

If the law asks us to share data, we will do so only as the law requires. We will tell you first when the law allows it.

3.3 Business Transfers

If this site is ever sold or merged, your data may move to the new owner. We will post a clear notice before any such change.

4. Data Security

We take fair steps to keep your data safe, such as secure hosting and limited access. No system is perfect, but we work to reduce risk.

4.1 Security Measures

We use secure connections and keep our software up to date. Access to any stored data is limited to the people who need it.

4.2 Data Breaches

If a breach ever happens, we will act fast to fix it and tell affected users. We will also tell the right authorities when the law asks.

4.3 Your Role

You can help by keeping your device and browser up to date. Do not share your passwords with anyone, on this site or elsewhere.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our site may use small cookies to remember your settings, such as your last used mode. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings.

We do not use cookies to track you across other sites. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy first.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to ask what data we hold about you. You can also ask us to fix it or delete it.

You can also ask us to stop using your data for any purpose. We will act on your request within a fair time, as the law requires.

7. Children's Privacy

Our tool is made for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Links

Our pages may link to other sites. Those sites have their own privacy rules, and we are not responsible for them.

Please read the privacy policy of any site you visit through our links. If a link looks wrong, tell us and we will check it.

9. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as it is needed for the reasons in this policy. Messages are kept only until your question is answered and closed.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous. Backup copies may stay for a short time, then they are removed too.

10. International Data Transfers

Our servers may sit in a different country than yours. When data moves across borders, we use fair safeguards to keep it protected.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the tool or the law changes. We will post the new version here and change the date at the top.

If a change is big, we will add a clear notice on the site. Please check this page from time to time.

12. Your Choices

You can use the tool without giving us any personal data. You can also clear cookies or use private mode in your browser at any time.

13. Analytics

We may use simple analytics to count visits and see which pages are used most. This data is grouped and cannot point to any one person.

14. Contact Information

If you have questions about this policy, or about your data, please use our Contact Form. We will reply as soon as we can.

15. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of Pakistan, where the site owner is based. Any dispute will be handled under those laws.
