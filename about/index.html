About Fix My Speaker

We build a free speaker cleaner that anyone can use, on any phone, in under two minutes flat.

Our Story

It started with a phone that fell in a pool. The speaker sounded dull for days, and each fix was slow or costly. Rice failed, and a shop wanted a high fee. We knew sound could do better.

We tested tones on wet phones. A low 165 Hz tone pushed water out well. Higher tones shook dust loose. We added vibration for bad cases and a stereo test for each side. The tool worked, so we put it online for free.

Today the tool helps people in many countries fix their speakers fast. It stays free, and we keep making it better with each new update.

Our Mission

Our mission is to give every phone user a fast way to fix a wet or dusty speaker, with no app.

The Science Behind Our Tool

Here is the simple science that makes our free tool work so well.

Web Audio API

The tool uses the Web Audio API, built into every modern browser. It makes clean sine tones with exact pitch and pans left or right. No files load, so the tool starts fast and works offline.

Water Eject Technology

A low 165 Hz tone shakes the membrane with strong, slow moves. The shake pushes water drops out of the grille until sound turns clear. It is the same idea smartwatches use.

Multi-Frequency Dust Removal

Dust has many sizes, so one tone fails. Our sweep plays six tones from 200 Hz to 1500 Hz, each shaking loose dirt of a new size. The sweep leaves the grille clean.

Vibration Enhancement

An 80 Hz bass tone pairs with the phone's vibration motor. Two forces work as one to shake out water that sound alone cannot move.

Stereo Control

Most phones have two speakers. Our tool can play sound on the left, the right, or both, so you can clean just the side that needs it.

Real-Time Processing

The tool shows progress as it runs. You see the percent done and the tone now playing, so you always know what is happening.
